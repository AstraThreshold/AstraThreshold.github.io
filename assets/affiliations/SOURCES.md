# Affiliation marks

Used to identify the owner’s university and internship, as requested. The website brand and favicon remain WoooLAB.

- `hkust-emblem.svg`: Exact emblem paths from the university’s official SVG, with the text paths excluded and a tight viewBox. Source: https://brand.hkust.edu.hk/profiles/ust/modules/custom/hkust_signature_affiliate/assets/images/hkust_logo_small.svg (linked by https://brand.hkust.edu.hk/). The shared university emblem is also used by HKUST(GZ).
- `brainco-source.png`: Unchanged transparent logo from https://www.brainco.tech/assets/brainco_logo-hvFOoEYh.png, referenced by the official website’s `brainco_logo-xkz_pAnA.js` module. CSS masks the symbol’s alpha channel, cropping out the wordmark. No resampling or generative reconstruction.
- The marks render at 23 px (21 px on phones) in the page’s foreground color, with transparent backgrounds. They automatically become light on a dark page and dark on a light page.
- Sources downloaded 2026-11-10; monochrome presentation added 2026-11-11 at the owner’s request.
